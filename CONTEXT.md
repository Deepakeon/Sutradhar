# Sutradhar

Platform for candidate and interviewer matching, mock technical and behavioral interviews, and confidence-building evaluation. See [PRODUCT.md](file:///home/deepak/Documents/Sutradhar/PRODUCT.md) for core product intent.

## Language

**Profile**:
A user's identity record holding personal information and platform details.
_Avoid_: User, Account, Member

**Candidate**:
The Profile taking the interview for practice and feedback.

**Interviewer**:
The Profile conducting the interview session and evaluating the Candidate.

**Interview**:
A 1-on-1 practice session between a Candidate and an Interviewer.

**Room**:
The real-time WebRTC audio and video interface where an Interview takes place.
