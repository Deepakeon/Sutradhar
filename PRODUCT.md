# Product Intent

## Core Goal

Sutradhar is an interview practice platform where people can both interview others and be interviewed.

## Focus Areas

- **Technical Interviews**: Live problem solving, system design, and coding discussions.
- **Behavioral Interviews**: Communication, situational scenarios, and STAR-method storytelling.

## Target Audience & Purpose

The application is built specifically for people who:
- Are not very good with interviews or lack interviewing experience.
- Experience interview anxiety and need a safe, constructive space to practice.
- Want to build up confidence, fluency, and practical experience by sitting on both sides of the interview table (as candidate and as interviewer).
